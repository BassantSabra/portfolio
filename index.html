Retail sales · Excel

Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Excel dashboard on Adidas US sales, covering customer behavior, regions, products and sales patterns, built to guide growth and expansion decisions.

Pivot TablesINDEX / MATCHIFSVBA macros
$120.2MTotal revenue
9,648Transactions

What the data showed

  • Seasonality. Sales peaked in April 2020 ($3.2M) and July 2021 ($10.4M), which informs production and inventory planning.
  • Gender split. Men (54% of revenue) lead in footwear; women (46%) lead in apparel. Promotions can be targeted by segment.
  • Region and channel. The West is the top region and Online is the top channel, ahead of In-store and Outlet.
  • Weekday pattern. Friday is the strongest day at $20M; Sunday and Monday are the weakest at $15–16M.
  • States. New York, California, Florida and Texas drive about 26% of revenue, while many Midwest and Mountain states contribute far less.

Method

  • Kaggle Adidas sales dataset with transactions, revenue, quantity, demographics, channel, region and product category.
  • Pivot tables for aggregation, dynamic formulas for calculations, and VBA macros to reset filters.

Logistics · Power BI

Fleet Management Dashboard

A three-page Power BI report on revenue, costs, driver performance and truck utilization, built to support decisions in logistics and fleet operations.

Power BIPower QueryDAXStar schemaSQL
$485MNet revenue
92KOrders
664KTons moved
456KKm traveled

Dashboard pages

  • Revenue and orders. Monthly trends show seasonal peaks from February to June, with breakdowns by truck type, trailer type and state.
  • Cost analysis. Total cost is $1.42M ($969K fixed, $448K variable), or $2.13 per ton and $3.10 per km. A scatter view relates distance to fuel and maintenance cost.
  • Drivers and trucks. Per-driver tracking of km, liters, fuel efficiency (km/L), fuel cost and maintenance cost, to spot efficient drivers and high-maintenance trucks.

Method

  • Power Query cleaning: removed duplicates and nulls, standardized names, fixed data types, handled missing truck usage and cost entries.
  • Star schema model and DAX measures for net revenue, year-over-year variance, fixed and variable cost, cost per km and per ton, and fuel efficiency.
